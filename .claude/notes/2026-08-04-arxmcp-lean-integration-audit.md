## 1. What arXMCP is today

arXMCP is a **local-first, loopback-only, single-user MCP retrieval server over a LanceDB corpus of parsed arXiv papers** — 8 frozen tools + 3 resources over Streamable HTTP at `127.0.0.1:7733/mcp/` (`server/_mcp_mount.py:34`, `server/main.py:918-931`), with a parallel unauthenticated 17-route mutation plane at `/ui/api` (`server/routes/notebooks.py`). It ships **no authentication of any kind** (`SECURITY.md:8`), **no OpenAPI** (`server/main.py:711-713`), and **no CI** (`.github/workflows/` NOT FOUND). Retrieval is **dense-only ANN** over BGE-M3 (`server/handlers/search.py:909-910`); BM25/RRF exists as modules but is not imported by the handler. The one genuinely strong asset is content-addressing: `chunk_id = arxiv:<paper_id>:<sha256(preamble_text + NFC(body_text))[:16]>` (`ingest/chunker.py:1277-1301`), source-SHA-frozen against edits (`tests/test_re_embed.py:773`). Three of eight tools have **no backing index on this machine**, and the server **cannot start with default config** — `var/arxmcp/index/lancedb/` does not exist; all corpora are per-notebook (`ARXMCP_NOTEBOOK=<slug>`, `server/config.py:541-646`).

## 2. The Lean integration surface today

**It is not a stub.** `server/handlers/lean_verify.py` is 1459 lines over a 483-line REPL harness (`server/lean_repl.py`), the largest handler in the repo, with nine commits of history.

**What it does:** takes an arbitrary agent-supplied snippet (≤16 KiB) plus up to 64 `import` lines, prepends them, and sends **one** round-trip to a long-lived `lake exe repl` subprocess (`server/lean_repl.py:230-244`). Three modes: `full` / `syntax_only` / `tactic_step` (`lean_verify.py:1181`). Supports `env` and `proof_state` continuation tokens so `import Mathlib` is paid once. On `mode=="full"` and `status in (ok, sorry)` it issues a **second** round-trip `{"cmd": "#print axioms <name>", "env": N}` and scores against

```python
AXIOM_ALLOWLIST = frozenset({"propext", "Quot.sound", "Classical.choice"})  # lean_verify.py:397-399
```

— **byte-identical** to the allowlist `bridgeland-stab-lean/formalization.yaml:82-90` claims for all 42 audited declarations. That is the one real semantic touchpoint between the two repos.

**What it does not do:** it has **no registry, no allowlist of artifacts, no notion of a trust record**. `_build_command` (`lean_verify.py:1002-1031`) passes the snippet through byte-for-byte in `full` mode. It never reads `formalization.yaml`, never reads `lean-toolchain` or `lakefile.toml`, and emits **no library identity** — grep for `mathlib_rev|toolchain|lean_version` over `server/` returns 4 hits, all comments/error strings. Its `env`/`proof_state` tokens are explicitly invalidated on respawn (`lean_verify.py:1205-1207`), so they are not citation keys.

**Real trust level — five independent problems:**

1. **Off by default.** `enable_lean=False`. Requires operator-supplied absolute `ARXMCP_LAKE_PATH` + `ARXMCP_LEAN_REPL_DIR`; **no lean-toolchain, lakefile, or repl commit pin exists anywhere in arXMCP.** The only record of what runs is prose: `plans/lean-verify-continuation/HANDOFF-2026-07-25-…-continuation.md:154-159` names repl `0cc6026` + Mathlib v4.31.0 + `leanprover/lean4:v4.31.0`, living at `C:\Users\cedar\lean-repl-spike\repl` **outside the repo**, recorded as detached HEAD with an uncommitted `lakefile.toml`. The Lean repo pins **v4.29.0** (`bridgeland-stab-lean/lean-toolchain`). **Version skew: arXMCP cannot check a bridgeland-stab-lean declaration in its own environment.**
2. **`status:"ok"` is not a trust verdict, by design and by arXMCP's own admission.** `.claude/roadmap-briefs/R3-verification-contract.md:4-12`: *"the current surface can be made to say `ok` to an `axiom`-backed proof."* The rename to `elaborated_no_errors` is queued (`plans/verification-contract/roadmap.yaml:117-145`, lane `now`) and **not shipped** — `_normalize_response` still sets `status="ok"` (`lean_verify.py:724`).
3. **A demonstrated fail-open in the axiom audit.** The declaration-name extractor (`_DECL_SITE_RE`/`_DECL_NAME_RE`, `lean_verify.py:445-456`) matches **neither** sites nor names for the legal single-line forms `set_option maxHeartbeats 400000 in theorem sneaky : False := bad` and `open Classical in theorem sneaky …`. Verification reproduced this by executing the regexes: `(['good'], True)` — `complete` stays `True`, the hidden declaration is never `#print axioms`-ed, and the record emits `outcome: "clean"`.
4. **Timeout is structurally too tight.** `DEFAULT_QUERY_TIMEOUT_S = 30.0` (`server/lean_repl.py:64`) bounds both round-trips. Cold `import Mathlib` measured **14.5 s – 235 s**. Any Mathlib-importing call times out, gets killed+respawned, pays cold cost again. Preload (`ARXMCP_LEAN_PRELOAD`) is the handoff doc's own "RESUME HERE" and is **NOT FOUND** in `server/`.
5. **No sandbox on this platform.** `RLIMIT_AS` is POSIX-only; Windows logs a WARN and skips it (`lean_repl.py:222-228`); the in-file comment at `:201-207` records the macOS path as **known broken** (issue #7). On Windows the 30 s wall clock is the only control over arbitrary agent-supplied Lean.

Plus a gate nobody documented: `server/tools.py:1005-1014` short-circuits **before dispatch** when `resources.bootstrap_mode_active`, returning `{"error_code":"no_notebook_selected"}` inside `CallToolResult(isError=True)` — a corpus-independent verifier hard-gated on corpus-ingest state, with a payload satisfying **zero** of the 13 `required` fields of the frozen schema.

**Tests — what they actually cover.** `tests/test_handlers_lean_verify.py` is 2791 lines across six axiom-audit test classes; the acceptance test is real (`:2402-2424` asserts `outcome=="flagged"`, `disallowed_axioms==["h"]`, the exact second command, and `"clean" not in json.dumps(result)`), and `:2426-2439` pins that `status`/`compilation_success` are **not** inferred from the audit. But:
- Every always-run test drives a `_FakeLeanRepl` with hand-queued dicts (`:74-123`). **CI-by-default exercises zero actual Lean** — Tier-3 is `@pytest.mark.requires_lean_repl`, skipped unless both env vars are set (`:56-62`).
- **No test calls the registered tool.** All call `handle_lean_verify` directly (`:186-188`); `_call_tool` is used for seven tools, never `lean_verify`. The observability wrapper and the bootstrap short-circuit are untested for it.
- On Windows the only RLIMIT test that runs asserts `"preexec_fn" not in captured` — i.e. **asserts the absence of a memory cap** (`:1028-1032`).
- The frozen `server/schemas/lean_verify_result.json` (v22) is **test-only**; the advertised wire `outputSchema` is the generic `{"type":"object","additionalProperties":true}` (reproduced against the repo venv). Clients get no machine-readable output contract.
- Zero adversarial fixtures. The only acceptance case is `axiom h : False`; the planned suite (target replacement, kind change, `unsafe`, `native_decide`, shadowing, cross-request leakage) is `plans/verification-contract/roadmap.yaml:250-265`, **not shipped**.

## 3. The notebook abstraction

**Mechanically:** a notebook is **a row in the `notebooks` table of `var/arxmcp/cache/notebooks.db`** (`server/notebooks_store.py`, `SCHEMA_VERSION = 5`) **plus a directory** `var/arxmcp/notebooks/<slug>/` (`tools/_notebook_common.py:33-41`), joined only by the slug string and the row's stored absolute `lancedb_path`. There is **no `NotebookSpec` type and no `notebook.yaml`** — grep returns nothing. Slug regex `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{2,30}$` (`_notebook_common.py:47`). Corpus dirs (`parse`/`chunk`/`embedding`/`raw`) are **global**; only `lancedb/` is per-notebook. Per-notebook state: `lancedb/corpus-version.json`, `index/bm25/v<N>/`, `cache/retrieval.db`, `documents.db`, `paper_metadata.db`, `queries.json`, `papers.txt`.

**Extension points: none.** No plugins, no hooks, no `entry_points`. `server/router_patterns.yaml` is loaded package-relative (`server/router.py:75`) — global. Chunker, embedder, and tool behavior cannot vary per notebook. The only knobs are data columns (`notebook_kind`, `discovery_category`, `description`), one namespaced settings key, and `queries.json`.

**Concrete steps to create one for a new topic:**
1. `make init NOTEBOOK=<slug> EMAIL=<addr>` → `tools/notebook_init.py` — scaffolds the dir, writes template `papers.txt` + `queries.json`, INSERTs the row via `NotebooksStore` (never raw sqlite3 — a direct INSERT leaves `user_version=0` and the v0→v1 migration **unconditionally DROPs the table**, `notebooks_store.py:162-164`). Sets `display_name=""` with no flag.
2. **Hand-edit `var/arxmcp/notebooks/<slug>/papers.txt`** with arXiv ids. No automation.
3. `python -m tools.notebook_fetch <slug>` — pull ar5iv HTML.
4. `python -m tools.notebook_ingest <slug>` — chunk/embed → `lancedb/`, then BM25. **This is the only ingest driver, and it imports exactly two things** (`build_bm25_index`, `run_bulk_ingest`, `tools/notebook_ingest.py:44-45`) — it builds **no** theorem-name, definition, or equation index.
5. Optional: `PATCH /ui/api/notebooks/<slug>/topic` to set `discovery_category` + `description` (else `tools/discover_for_notebook.py` raises).
6. Optional: hand-author `queries.json` eval fixtures.
7. Serve with `ARXMCP_NOTEBOOK=<slug>` or query with `filters={"notebook":"<slug>"}`.

Steps 2, 5, 6 are silently skippable — which is exactly what happened to `bridgeland-stability`: after 13 ingest runs it has `display_name=""`, `discovery_category=""`, `description=""`, and `queries.json` is still the generated `EXAMPLE-q1` template.

**Two structural defects.** (a) **Split membership:** `POST /ui/api/notebooks/<slug>/papers` writes the `notebook_papers` junction and never touches `papers.txt`; ingest reads only `papers.txt`. Live: `notebook_papers` has **0 rows repo-wide** while `bridgeland-stability/papers.txt` has 146 — so `arxmcp://notebooks/bridgeland-stability` reports `paper_count: 0` alongside `is_ingested: true`. (b) `make add` branches on whether `/healthz` answers (`Makefile:530-547`), producing two non-converging effects from one command. Also: `tools/notebook_restore.py` — the nominal clone path — writes the `notebooks` table by **raw sqlite3** (`:225,302,320`) with no `user_version` handling, re-arming the DROP hazard.

## 4. Identifier durability — the load-bearing question

**NO — a Lean declaration cannot durably cite an arXMCP *statement* today.** But the answer is more nuanced than "no", and the nuance matters.

**What is genuinely strong.** `chunk_id` is a real content hash, not a rowid: `sha256(preamble_text + NFC(body_text))[:16]` (`ingest/chunker.py:1277-1301`), with two-run and cross-process determinism tests (`tests/test_chunker_ids.py:140,573`) and a source-SHA freeze on the function body (`tests/test_re_embed.py:773`). For **math/0212237 specifically**, all 66 chunks have `preamble_ref = NULL`, so `preamble_text = ""` and the id reduces to `sha256(NFC(body_text))[:16]`. Verification **recomputed both hashes and they match**:

- Lemma 8.2 → `arxiv:math/0212237:a82c3230040fd724`
- Proposition 8.1 → `arxiv:math/0212237:3d821c479c6f091d`

`sanitize_retrieved_text()` is byte-identity on both bodies, so a caller can verify offline: unwrap `<retrieved_chunk>`, NFC-normalize, re-hash, compare. **This works for 1231/15280 rows (8.06%) corpus-wide** — exactly the `preamble_ref IS NULL` set. It is an accident of failed preamble extraction, not a designed guarantee.

**Why it still fails as a durable citation:**

1. **Rotation is a routine, first-party operation.** The repo's own `Makefile` help for `ingest-recover-preambles` reads *"NOTE: triggers chunk_id rotation; follow with make re-embed-all."* Any chunker bump, ar5iv re-render, LaTeXML upgrade, or HTML→MinerU migration mints new ids.
2. **No forwarding.** NOT FOUND: any alias table, redirect, or `previous_chunk_id` column in the 26-column `CHUNKS_SCHEMA_V1` (`ingest/schema.py:133-272`).
3. **Rotation doubles rather than fails.** `merge_insert` has `when_matched_update_all` + `when_not_matched_insert_all` and **no delete arm** (`ingest/store.py:907-911`; zero `delete` occurrences in the file). Stale rows stay addressable until a whole-dataset swap.
4. **The id is namespace-ambiguous.** `equation_id` uses the **identical** shape `arxiv:<paper_id>:<16hex>` (`ingest/extract_equations.py:101`) and passes `is_valid_chunk_id`. A bare `arxiv:…:<16hex>` does not name its table.
5. **The id does not name its corpus.** Notebooks are separate LanceDB datasets with independent `corpus_version` (5048 / 99 / 209 / 85 live). **No tool emits the notebook slug.** A usable pin needs (slug, corpus_version, table, id); only one of four is emitted near the data.
6. **`corpus_version` is not a content commitment.** It is the LanceDB MVCC integer; it increments on metadata-only merges and a restore-from-backup presents a **lower** version over different bytes (`server/corpus.py:1-15`).
7. **`source_span` — the designed durable anchor — is 100% NULL.** `ingest/store.py:598-600` writes it as literal `None` on every new chunk ("forward-wiring is a tracked fast-follow"); the offline backfill always emits `id:""`; and **no handler accepts a `source_span` or txt-hash as input**. There is no resolve-by-content-hash entrypoint in `server/handlers/`.
8. **Statements are not first-class objects.** No statements table. Document position is discarded — the chunker assigns a monotonic `idx<N>` then overwrites it at `ingest/chunker.py:1194`; `server/proof_linkage.py:19-20` states verbatim: *"No column of `CHUNKS_SCHEMA_V1` records document position."*

**The durable coordinate that does exist:** `(arXiv id, printed_number, corpus-manifest content_hash)`. `printed_number` is chunker-native (`ingest/chunker.py:505`), persisted (`ingest/store.py:597`), served by `get_chunk` (`server/handlers/chunk.py:206`) — 36 of the 66 math/0212237 chunks carry one, including `8.1` and `8.2`. `content_hash` (`server/corpus_manifest.py:126-141`) is a real sha256 over canonical JSON, but it hashes **registry rows** (`work_id, arxiv_version, parse_artifact_sha256, raw_source_sha256, license_status, status`), not chunk bodies, and lives on an MCP **resource** unreachable from any tool result. **No tool accepts `printed_number` as an input** — there is no "fetch the chunk numbered 8.2 in math/0212237" path.

**The Lean side today** pins only `source.id: math/0212237`, `type: arXiv` (`bridgeland-stab-lean/formalization.yaml:31`). A repo-wide grep of all 15 tracked files for `arxmcp|arxiv:|chunk_id|corpus_version|printed_number|source_span` returns **zero hits**. **There is no cross-repo identifier of any kind.**
