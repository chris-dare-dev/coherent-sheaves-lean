## New implementation

### Genuine quadratic forms

`BridgelandStabLean/StabilityCondition/Support/Quadratic.lean`

- `quadraticForm_isHomogTwo` proves an actual `QuadraticForm ℝ V` is
  continuous and degree-two homogeneous in finite dimension.
- `HasQuadraticSupportProperty` quantifies over a bundled quadratic form.
- `HasQuadraticSupportProperty.hasSupportProperty` connects it to the existing
  norm-bound support predicate.

There is deliberately no converse over an arbitrary normed space: the
existing norm-square witness need not be quadratic without an inner-product
norm.

### One form across an indexed family

`BridgelandStabLean/StabilityCondition/Support/Uniform.lean`

- `HasUniformQuadraticSupportProperty` has quantifier order
  `∃ Q, (∀ i, nonnegative on S i) ∧ negative on ker Z`.
- It is equivalent to quadratic support on `⋃ i, S i`.
- Constant families, reindexing, and two-way linear-equivalence transport are
  proved using the same witness.

The empty-index case still requires a form negative on `ker Z`; uniformity is
therefore not vacuous.

### Integral and real quotients

`BridgelandStabLean/StabilityCondition/Support/ZeroChargeLattice.lean`

- constructs the smallest saturated subgroup containing a set of integral
  classes;
- proves the quotient torsion-free;
- proves a finitely generated quotient is free abelian;
- descends a charge that vanishes on the generators.

`BridgelandStabLean/StabilityCondition/Support/Quotient.lean`

- descends a real-linear charge through `V ⧸ V₀`;
- maps every indexed selected locus into the quotient;
- puts one genuine quadratic form on that quotient;
- recovers the norm support property on each quotient locus.

### Binding to weak semistability

`BridgelandStabLean/StabilityCondition/Weak/Support/Quadratic.lean`

- `QuadraticSupportData` binds the form to
  `WeakStabilityFunction.semistableClasses` and records exact charge
  compatibility;
- `UniformQuadraticSupportData` uses one charge and one form across an indexed
  family;
- `QuotientUniformQuadraticSupportData` additionally requires every
  zero-charge heart class to lie in the killed subspace and places the form on
  the quotient.
